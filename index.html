The mechanism

One trade, four turns of the loop

01

Buy

Every $LOOP swap feeds the engine. A slice of each trade's fees is collected and routed into the loop.

02

Deploy

Those fees fund arbitrage pools alongside promising projects - tight ranges that attract real trading volume.

03

Harvest

Traders arb the spread across those pools. The loop captures the flow block by block, compounding it on the way.

04

Sweep

Profits convert to $SOL and are paid out to holders. Rebalance the pools, pay holders, repeat.

Answers

FAQ

What is $LOOP?

$LOOP uses a portion of creator fees to fund arbitrage pools with promising projects, in order to stimulate volume and bring $SOL to holders.

Where does the yield actually come from?

Swap fees. Every trader paying spread to move size across a pool is the revenue source. That makes the yield tied to real activity, not printed emissions.

Where do the fees go?

A portion of fees is deployed into arbitrage pools alongside promising projects, which stimulates trading volume. The holder share is paid out in $SOL; the dev share funds development and maintenance, arbitrage pool capital, audits and security, infrastructure, and marketing and listings.

How do I get paid as a holder?

The sweep step of the loop pays harvested fees to eligible holder wallets in $SOL. Details and the claims dashboard ship with Phase 1.
