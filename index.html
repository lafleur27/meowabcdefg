The mechanism

One trade, four turns of the loop

01

Buy

Every $LOOP swap feeds the engine. A slice of each trade is routed into working liquidity instead of sitting idle.

02

Deploy

Capital splits across dozens of micro pools on tight pairs, so depth is everywhere and slippage is nowhere.

03

Harvest

Traders pay spread to move size. The loop collects that spread block by block, compounding it on the way.

04

Sweep

Profits rotate back out: rebalance the pools, pay holders, repeat. The circle never has an exit.

Answers

FAQ

What is $LOOP?

$LOOP is a project that uses a portion of creator fees to create arbitrage pools with promising projects, in order to stimulate volume and bring $SOL to holders.

Where does the yield actually come from?

Swap fees. Every trader paying spread to move size across a pool is the revenue source. That makes the yield tied to real activity, not printed emissions.

What are micro pools and why not one big pool?

Small pools concentrated on tight price ranges capture far more fees per dollar of liquidity than a wide, lazy pool. The mesh of them covers the whole board.

How do I get paid as a holder?

The sweep step of the loop distributes harvested fees to eligible holder wallets. Details and the claims dashboard ship with Phase 1.

Is the contract audited?

Launch happens with the audit and locked liquidity - check the contract address above before buying anything, and only trust links from this site's footer.
